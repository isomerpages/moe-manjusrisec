<p>We are proud to announce that five of our student leaders were each conferred
the prestigious award by the Marine Parade Leadership Foundation. &nbsp;The
recipients were Allam Rithwick Reddy, Charlene Cheok Eu Xing, Lua En Qi
Celine, Tay Qi Xuan and Yip Ler Quan Gareth who are all from the cohort
that graduated in December 2025.&nbsp; Their family members were in the
audience to witness the ceremony held at MacPherson Community Club on 26
July 2026.</p>
<p>The award is presented to those who had demonstrated exemplary leadership
in school and/or community activities in the past year; and comes with
a certificate, plaque and cash cheque.&nbsp; The awards are named for Singapore’s
former Prime Minister Mr Goh Chok Tong; and the certificates were signed
by Mr Seah Kian Peng, Singapore’s Speaker of Parliament.</p>
